Established in 1865, the United States Secret Service is a federal law enforcement organization with a dual mandate: protection and investigative missions.

Explore Our Mission Career Opportunities

Our Dual Mission

Executed with unwavering integrity, vigilance, and service.

Protection

Safeguarding the President, Vice President, their families, visiting foreign heads of state, and major national events. We secure the White House complex, foreign diplomatic missions, and designated critical infrastructure.

  • Presidential & VP Security Detail
  • National Special Security Events (NSSE)
  • White House & Foreign Mission Security

Investigations

Defending the nation's financial and economic infrastructure against complex international crimes, including cyberattacks, financial fraud, counterfeiting, and transnational organized crime networks.

  • Cyber & Network Intrusion Investigations
  • Financial Crimes & Fraud Task Forces
  • Counterfeiting & Currency Protection

Leadership

Guiding the agency with dedication and absolute adherence to the constitutional oath.
